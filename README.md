# 💇 Hair Inspo

Transform your look with AI-powered hairstyling and virtual hair color previews.

**Hair Inspo** helps you discover new hairstyles and experiment with different hair colors before visiting the salon. Simply upload your photo and explore personalized styles to find the perfect look that matches your personality.

## ✨ Features

### 💇 Hairstyling
- Try different hairstyles
- AI-powered hairstyle previews
- Personalized styling suggestions

### 🎨 Hair Color
- Change hair color instantly
- Preview multiple shades
- Explore trendy and classic colors

## 🚀 Why Hair Inspo?

Choosing a new hairstyle or hair color can be challenging. Hair Inspo lets you preview different looks in seconds, helping you make confident styling decisions before making any permanent changes.

## 📱 Key Features

- 🤖 AI-powered hairstyle generation
- 💇 Virtual hairstyle preview
- 🎨 Hair color changer
- 📸 Photo upload support
- ⚡ Fast and user-friendly interface
- ✨ Realistic styling results

## 🛠️ Benefits

- Experiment without commitment
- Find styles that suit your face
- Explore trendy hairstyles and colors
- Save time before salon visits
- Easy-to-use experience
